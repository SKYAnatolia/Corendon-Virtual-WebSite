WELCOME TO CORENDON VIRTUAL

Our community's goal is not just to fly; it's to experience planned flights, share our

experiences, and grow together. We offer a better environment for you, our valued pilots.

In Corendon Virtual, the pilot is a part of a disciplined operational approach and

contributes to making virtual aviation more enjoyable and procedural.

A special message for you from our CEO

"Dear Pilots,

First of all, welcome to Corendon Virtual. Whether you are a professional pilot or a new user on Infinite Flight, we are happy and excited to see you as part of our family. Because we have much work to do together on this long and challenging journey.

As you all know, Corendon Airlines, although one of Türkiye's low-cost airlines, is one of the companies that has made a name for itself by flying to many regions of Europe. We decided to bring this company to Infinite Flight. With this decision, we are helping you gain realistic pilot experiences by transferring Corendon Airlines' real-world flights into simulation.

As a pilot on Corendon Virtual, you will have access to various resources, including detailed flight operations, support from our dedicated staff, and regular events designed to enhance your flying experience. We are working to make your time with us as productive as possible and are always ready to support you in realizing your aviation dreams.

We look forward to seeing you in the sky and can't wait to fly with you as part of Corendon Virtual!

We wish you safe flights."

Sami_Yusuf_Beslenen
CEO & Founder, Corendon Virtual

About Us

Who are we?

When we founded Corendon, our core values were discipline and fun. What started as a small community has led us to become a well-known name today.

Our team is honored to contribute to Infinite Flight with Corendon, a low-cost airline. By considering our diverse perspectives, we can help you have a better flying experience.
